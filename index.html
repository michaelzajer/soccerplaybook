How it works

Three taps from idea to session

No stylus, no laptop, no course to complete. It is built for a phone in one hand at the side of a pitch.

Lay out the drill

Drag cones, balls, goals and numbered players onto the pitch. Everything snaps to an invisible grid, so your square actually looks square.

Draw the movement

Sketch passes, runs and dribbles with a finger. Wobbly lines straighten themselves and the ends clip neatly onto the nearest cone or ball.

Press play

The app works out the order on its own — passes into space, a player running onto a pass, and everyone rotating back through the queue.

FAQ

Questions coaches ask

Is My Soccer Coach free?

Yes — a completely free toolkit built by coaches, for coaches. You can use it without creating an account at all; you only need one if you want your squad and drills saved and synced.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. It installs to your phone and keeps working with no signal, which matters on most grassroots pitches. Anything you change syncs again once you are back in range.

Can I animate my own drills?

Yes, and that is the point of it. Place your cones and players, draw the passes and runs, then press play. The app works out who does what and in what order — including a pass into space, a player running onto a pass, and the rotation back to the end of the queue.

Can I track substitutions during the game?

Yes. Game day gives you a bench, an out-of-play zone for anyone injured or unavailable, a match clock per period and an independent subs timer.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your phone's browser. If you want it on your home screen like a normal app, use "Add to Home Screen" and it will open full screen and work offline.
