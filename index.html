Frequently Asked Questions

Is My Soccer Coach free?

Yes! My Soccer Coach is a completely free toolkit built by coaches, for coaches.

Does the app work offline?

Absolutely. We know reception at the pitch can be terrible. Once loaded, the app works entirely offline, so your tactics board and game clock will never let you down.

Can I track substitutions during the game?

Yes. The Game Day Manager includes a dedicated subs timer and out-of-play zones so you can accurately track exactly how many minutes each player has been on the field.
