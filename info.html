My SoccerCoach

App Information & Features

My Soccer Coach is a comprehensive digital toolkit designed specifically for grassroots soccer coaches.

Key Features

  • Tactics Board: Quickly arrange your starting XI and visualize formations with easy drag-and-drop mechanics.
  • Drills Planner: Build training sessions by plotting cones, goals, players, and passing lines directly on a virtual pitch.
  • Game Day Manager: The ultimate sideline companion. Track match time with a digital clock and manage your substitutions perfectly with our dedicated subs timer. Never lose track of who needs minutes again!
  • Offline Ready: Poor reception at the pitch? No problem. The app works completely offline once loaded.

Getting Started

Simply click the "Open Web App" button on the home page. You can use it as a guest immediately, or sign up for a free account to save your rosters and settings securely in the cloud.
