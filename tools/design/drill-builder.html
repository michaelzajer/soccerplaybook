Why this shape of screen

  1. The preview is the point. It sits above the controls and redraws on every change, so the coach never has to imagine the outcome. This is the one thing a drag-and-drop board cannot give you — an instant, correct layout.
  2. Three groups, in the order a coach thinks. Markers, then players, then what happens. That is how you described it, and it is how you would brief an assistant.
  3. Metres, not pixels. The size slider is in real distances because a coach knows "12 to 15 metres", not "0.2 of the board".
  4. It writes the notation. Nothing new to maintain: the screen is a front end to the parser that already exists, so anything the builder makes can also be typed, shared as text, or generated by me.
  5. Drawing stays. "Step by step…" drops into the existing draw tools for combination play like complex3. The builder handles shapes and patterns; the pencil handles the rest.
