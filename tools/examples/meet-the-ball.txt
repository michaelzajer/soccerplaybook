# "Player 1 passes to player 2, who runs out 5m to meet the ball and then
#  passes to player 3 who is at cone 3."
#
# This is the worked example of prose -> notation. Every clause in that sentence
# lands somewhere explicit:
#
#   "passes to player 2 ... who runs out 5m to meet the ball"
#       the pass does NOT end on player 2's cone. It ends at the meeting point,
#       5 m off his cone towards the passer, and player 2 runs to the same
#       point. Two lines ending in the same place is what the engine reads as
#       "arrive together" (rule 3b) — the pass first, then the run.
#
#   "then passes to player 3"
#       drawn after the run, so it waits for player 2 to get there and for the
#       ball to arrive (rules 1 and 2).
#
#   "who is at cone 3"
#       just tells us where player 3 is, which the GRID already says. It is not
#       an action, so it produces no line.
