About My Soccer Coach

My Soccer Coach was born out of frustration and necessity. As a soccer dad and volunteer coach, I found myself juggling clipboards, stopwatches, messy substitution sheets, and scattered drill diagrams every single weekend.

I realized that managing the game on the sidelines shouldn't distract from actually coaching the kids. We needed a tool that was simple, offline-capable, and designed for the real-life issues coaches face on game day.

That's why I built My Soccer Coach. It brings everything you need—tactics boards, drill planners, and a game day manager with live substitution timers—into one beautifully designed, easy-to-use app.

Whether you're running a weekend rec league team or a competitive academy squad, My Soccer Coach is designed to give you back your time on the touchline.
